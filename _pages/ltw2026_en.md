PLACEHOLDER — Opening: statewide turnout rose from 60.3 to 77.8 per cent. Where exactly?

### Where Turnout Rose the Most

PLACEHOLDER — The increase is largest where turnout was lowest in 2021: shrinking, sparsely populated, older municipalities far from the nearest large city.
